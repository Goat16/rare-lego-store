RARE BRICK VAULT – STARTER STORE

Files:
- index.html
- styles.css
- script.js

How to preview:
1. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.

Before publishing:
1. In script.js replace both instances of YOUR_EMAIL_HERE with your selling email address.
2. Replace the sample product data at the top of script.js with your real LEGO items.
3. Replace the mock LEGO graphics with your actual item photos when ready.
4. Decide whether you want inquiry-based sales, Stripe checkout, PayPal, or a Shopify-powered checkout.

This is a static starter site. It does not process credit cards yet.
